Good questions

Everything else, answered short.

What happens right after I subscribe?

Premium turns on immediately. Your first mystery deal appears on the home screen, early access starts with the next evening reveal, and points begin tripling on your next redemption.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, from Settings in two taps. You keep member pricing until the end of the period you already paid for, then return to the free plan with your saved deals intact.

Do reward points expire?

Points last twelve months from the day you earn them, and they can be spent on any deal in the app. We send a note before any of them are about to lapse.

Is Premium available outside Riyadh?

Riyadh today, Jeddah and Dammam next. Your membership travels with you, so you can use it in any city we open.

What if a restaurant cancels my deal?

We refund the full amount to your wallet within three working days, and points are returned. If it happens twice at the same kitchen, we take them off the list.

How secure is checkout?

Payments run through a PCI-compliant provider. Card numbers never touch our servers, and we do not store them on your profile.
