How do I redeem a deal?

Open the deal, tap Add to my deals, then show the code at the till. The partner scans it once and the discount comes off the bill. Codes are single-use and tied to your account.

When do new deals appear?

The full list refreshes at 8:00 AM and the evening reveal lands at 6:00 PM. Premium members see the mystery two hours before everyone else.

What exactly is the mystery deal?

One hand-picked offer a day, with a bigger discount than the usual list. You see the kitchen and the price before you claim it; the extra courses stay a surprise until you sit down.

Why did a deal disappear?

Partners cap each drop and can pause it when the kitchen fills up. If it is gone before you claim it, it usually returns within the week. Saved deals stay in your deck either way.

How do I cancel Premium?

Settings, then Premium, then Cancel plan. You keep the benefits until the end of the period you have already paid for, then return to the free plan with your saved deals intact.

When am I charged?

Billing starts on the day you subscribe, then renews monthly or yearly on the same date. We email a receipt every time. If a renewal fails, we retry once after 24 hours before pausing the plan.

Do reward points expire?

Points last twelve months from the day you earn them and can be spent on any deal in the app. We send a note before any are about to lapse.

How do I change my city?

Head to Settings and choose a city. Deals and mystery picks update on the next refresh, and your saved deals stay available.

How do I delete my data?

Email privacy@flipdeal.sa from the address on your account. We remove your profile, saved deals and preferences within seven days and confirm when it is done.

How do I become a partner?

Send the short form on the partners page. We onboard in Riyadh first, then Jeddah and Dammam. There is no listing fee and you approve every drop before it runs.
