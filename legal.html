Privacy

Flip Deal is built around your city and the deals you save. We collect as little as we can and we do not sell any of it.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and city, when you create an account.
  • The deals you save, redeem and rate, so recommendations can improve.
  • Your plan and billing status. Card details stay with our payment provider.
  • Basic device and usage data, used to keep the app stable and to prevent abuse.

What we do not collect

We do not store your location history, your contacts or your browsing outside Flip Deal. The city you choose is enough to show nearby deals.

Who sees it

Partner restaurants see a redemption code, not your profile. Our payment provider sees what it needs to process a charge. Nobody else sees your data unless the law requires it.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data at any time by writing to privacy@flipdeal.sa. We confirm deletions within seven days. Marketing email can be turned off in Settings without affecting your account.

Terms

These terms cover the Flip Deal app and website, the deals listed on them and any Premium membership you buy.

Using Flip Deal

You need an account to save or redeem a deal. Keep it yours: codes are single-use and tied to your profile. We may suspend accounts that resell codes or abuse partner staff.

Deals and partners

  • Deals are supplied by independent restaurants and can change or sell out before you claim them.
  • The listed price is the price you pay, including VAT unless stated otherwise.
  • Partners set their own availability, dietary information and service standards.
  • If a partner cancels a redeemed deal, we refund it to your Flip Deal wallet within three working days.

Premium and billing

Premium costs 49 SAR per month or 399 SAR per year. It renews automatically until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current period stays active. Prices can change with 30 days’ notice.

Refunds and liability

Unused deals can be refunded to your wallet within 24 hours of purchase. Flip Deal is a marketplace, not the restaurant: the kitchen is responsible for the food it serves. Nothing in these terms removes rights you have under Saudi consumer law.

Contact

Questions about these terms go to legal@flipdeal.sa, or use the help centre for anything about a specific deal.
